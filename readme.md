**Gorilla pas** is a modern web-based arcade artillery game inspired directly by the legendary **Gorillas.bas** QBasic MS-DOS classic from the early 1990s. Rebuilt using vanilla HTML5 Canvas and the Web Audio API, this version brings back the nostalgic retro feel while introducing enhanced ballistic physics, customizable gravity, and a smart AI opponent.

---

## 🎮 Features

- **QBasic Heritage & Authentic Audio:** Features a synthesized intro melody faithfully recreated to match the classic PC Speaker start-up tunes.
- **Game Modes:** 
  - **1 Player vs IA BAS:** Challenge a built-in AI opponent equipped with real-time ballistic calculation and adaptive learning.
  - **2 Players:** Local head-to-head multiplayer mode.
- **Customizable Physics:** Adjust wind resistance and gravity on the fly (Earth `9.8`, Mars `3.7`, Moon `1.6`, or **Zero Gravity** mode with an outer space cutscene).
- **Destructible Environment:** Buildings feature glowing windows and dynamic craters left behind by exploding bananas or blast shockwaves.
- **Classic Easter Eggs:** Leave the game idle to watch the Sun/Moon fall asleep (complete with nightcap and floating Zs) while *Galaga/Galaxian* retro spaceships fly across the sky.
- **Clean UI / HUD:** Player banners in the top corners featuring pixel-art gorilla avatars, live scores, and active turn indicators.
- **SEO Optimized:** Fully tagged with meta keywords (`juego qbasic`, `juego online`, `gorillas bas`, `gorilla pas`, `arcade`, `retro`) for search discoverability.

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
2. Open the `index.html` file in any modern web browser.
3. Click **"INICIAR GORILLA PAS"** to activate the audio synthesizer.
4. Follow the prompts to configure your match (Game mode, player names, total rounds, and gravity).
5. Enter your **Angle** (0°–180°) and **Velocity** (1–150) in the controls panel.
6. Click **"¡LANZAR BANANO!"** or press **Enter** to fire. Account for wind speed and clear the buildings to hit your opponent!
